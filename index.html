Clinical Experiences Summary

Advanced Internal Medicine

Hospital of the University of Pennsylvania (HUP) | Fall 2025 | 65 Clinical Hours

My clinical experience on Founders 11 focused on the management of hospitalized adult patients with complex acute and chronic medical conditions. Responsibilities included obtaining comprehensive and focused histories, performing physical examinations, reviewing laboratory and diagnostic studies, developing differential diagnoses, and presenting patient cases to attending physicians and advanced practice providers. This rotation strengthened my ability to integrate advanced pathophysiology, pharmacology, and evidence-based practice into the care of medically complex patients while collaborating with interdisciplinary healthcare teams.

Cardiology

Penn Presbyterian Medical Center | Spring 2026 | 160 Clinical Hours

During my cardiology rotation, I cared for patients with acute and chronic cardiovascular conditions including heart failure, coronary artery disease, arrhythmias, hypertension, and other cardiac disorders. Clinical activities included interpretation of electrocardiograms, assessment of hemodynamic status, medication management, patient education, and participation in multidisciplinary rounds. This experience enhanced my understanding of cardiovascular pathophysiology and strengthened my clinical reasoning skills in the evaluation and management of cardiac patients.

Heart & Vascular Intensive Care Unit (HVICU)

Penn Presbyterian Medical Center | Spring 2026 | 160 Clinical Hours

The HVICU rotation provided extensive exposure to critically ill cardiovascular patients requiring advanced monitoring and intensive care management. I participated in the care of patients with complex cardiac conditions, postoperative cardiovascular surgery patients, and critically ill individuals requiring invasive monitoring and advanced therapies. Clinical experiences included interpretation of hemodynamic data, assessment of critically ill patients, development of treatment plans, and collaboration with physicians, advanced practice providers, nurses, pharmacists, and other healthcare professionals. This rotation strengthened my ability to manage high-acuity patients and apply advanced clinical decision-making in critical care settings.

Advanced Lung Disease Service

Hospital of the University of Pennsylvania (HUP) | Summer 2026 | 240 Clinical Hours (Ongoing)

My Advanced Lung Disease rotation has provided extensive experience managing patients with complex pulmonary diseases including cystic fibrosis, bronchiectasis, chronic respiratory failure, pulmonary infections, and advanced lung disease. Clinical responsibilities include conducting comprehensive patient assessments, reviewing diagnostic studies, developing differential diagnoses, evaluating treatment effectiveness, and presenting patient cases to attending physicians.

Notable clinical experiences have included the management of patients with cystic fibrosis exacerbations complicated by multidrug-resistant Pseudomonas aeruginosa, chronic hypoxemic respiratory failure, bronchiectasis, pulmonary MAC infection, and acute kidney injury. Through these experiences, I have developed proficiency in antibiotic stewardship, pulmonary disease management, interpretation of advanced imaging and laboratory data, and coordination of care among pulmonology, infectious disease, pharmacy, respiratory therapy, nutrition, and rehabilitation teams.

This rotation has significantly enhanced my ability to synthesize advanced specialty knowledge, apply evidence-based interventions, and provide comprehensive care to medically complex adult patients.
